This is the personal website of {{ site.name }}, a painter based in {{ site.studio }}. It's deliberately simple: **no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.**

## If you send a message

When you use the contact form, the details you enter (your name, email address and message) are sent through [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy), a form service that delivers them to me by email. I use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of our conversation. I never sell or share your details.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, to keep the service secure.

## Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, at any time. Just [get in touch](/contact/).
